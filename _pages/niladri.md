**Niladri S. Chatterji** is a researcher at OpenAI. Previously, he was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University working with Tatsu Hashimoto and Percy Liang, and he completed his PhD at UC Berkeley advised by Peter Bartlett. His research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and statistics, with a current focus on building more robust language models. In the past, he has worked on interpolating models, optimization theory, online learning, and MCMC algorithms.
