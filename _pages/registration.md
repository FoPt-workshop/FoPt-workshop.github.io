Registration for FoPt 2026 is automatically included in the registration fee for COLT 2026.

### Registration procedure
1. To register for COLT 2026, please visit [the registration website](https://learningtheory.org/colt2026/), go to the 'Pre-register' tab and create an account. You will receive an email with a link to confirm your account.
2. Please sign in with your email address and password. Go to the Pre-register tab, accept the terms of the contract and complete your registration.
3. Please select your options and payment.
